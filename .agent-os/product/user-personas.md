# User Personas - MusicBrainz Explorer

## Primary Persona: Dr. Sarah Chen - Music Industry Researcher

### Demographics
- **Age**: 35
- **Role**: Assistant Professor, Music Industry Studies
- **Location**: University of Southern California, Los Angeles
- **Education**: PhD in Musicology, MBA in Business

### Background & Context
Dr. Chen researches the evolution of independent record labels and their impact on genre development. She regularly publishes academic papers on music industry consolidation patterns and teaches courses on music business analytics. Her research requires deep analysis of label relationships, artist migration patterns, and release timeline analysis.

### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Analyze label consolidation patterns in specific genres (indie rock, electronic music)
- **Research Objective**: Track how artist roster changes correlate with label ownership changes
- **Publication Goal**: Generate data-driven insights for peer-reviewed publications
- **Teaching Goal**: Create case studies and datasets for student projects

### Pain Points with Traditional Tools
- **Data Fragmentation**: Information scattered across multiple databases and websites
- **Limited Relationship Mapping**: Difficult to visualize complex label hierarchies
- **Export Limitations**: Most music databases don't provide research-grade data export
- **Time-Intensive**: Manual data gathering takes weeks for comprehensive studies

### How MusicBrainz Explorer Solves Her Problems
- **Visual Family Trees**: Instantly understand complex label ownership structures
- **Comprehensive Export**: JSON/CSV export for statistical analysis in R/Python
- **Historical Analysis**: Track label and artist relationships over time
- **Integrated Discovery**: Find related entities through visual relationship mapping

### Usage Patterns
- **Deep Research Sessions**: 2-3 hour exploration sessions, 2-3 times per week
- **Systematic Analysis**: Follows label hierarchies and documents findings methodically
- **Data Export**: Regularly exports datasets for analysis in statistical software
- **Collaboration**: Shares findings and playlists with research colleagues

### Success Metrics
- Reduces research data gathering time from weeks to days
- Discovers previously unknown label relationships for research papers  
- Creates comprehensive datasets that support statistical analysis
- Generates shareable playlists that demonstrate musical relationships
